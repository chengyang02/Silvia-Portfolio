Art Projects

Activism

The social issue that I chose was animal protection. I got the idea by looking at Artist Sue Coe, an artist focusing on animal protection. And I think representing the issue with art could raise awareness about many issues, such as animal cruelty, poaching, habitat destruction, and the importance of conservation. Art grabs people’s attention quickly, and people directly see the information, and they will be reminded that they should not do something that will cause this problem.

Working Class

The theme of my artist is about working class in the 1880, so I choose to present people working today, addressing different opinion and lifestyle that people have today. Some of the sketches present the feeling of clam and harmony other might present the working pressure that people have, and the suffocated feeling of fast speed life. The final idea that I had chose is idea 5, I used different elements and methods to present working load that people are facing.

Identity: Fear

The theme of the project is identity, presenting identity using soft materials. In my initial sketches, I drew things that reflect both my personal growth and the essence of who I am. Some of the sketches depict the distance between me and others, some symbolize the fears l've overcome, and some show my personality though using of colors. In the background, dream-like symbols represent past uncertainties. These drawings illustrate my journey from vulnerability to strength, showing how I've grown and what matters most.
